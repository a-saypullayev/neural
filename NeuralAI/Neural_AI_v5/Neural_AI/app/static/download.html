Neural AI for Windows

Get Neural AI as a Windows app. It opens in its own window, with no tabs or address bar, and looks exactly like the website.

Download for Windows

NeuralAI.exe · about 60 KB · Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) · needs Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome

How to install

  1. Click Download for Windows and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows warns about every new unsigned program.
  3. Neural AI opens in its own window. To keep it handy, right-click its taskbar icon and choose Pin to taskbar, or move NeuralAI.exe to your Desktop.

How it works

The app is a small launcher that opens this site in an app-style window using Edge or Chrome. Your chats stay in your account on the server, so you can sign in on any device with the same username and password. An internet connection is required.

Other devices
