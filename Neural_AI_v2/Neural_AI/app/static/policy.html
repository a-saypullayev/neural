Privacy policy

We collect only what the service needs: your username, a salted password hash, your chats, the memories you save, usage counters (requests and characters per day), and API key metadata. We do not sell your data. Chats and memories are tied to your account and every query is scoped to it.

Messages you send are processed by the AI model configured by the operator of this deployment to produce replies. If you turn on Search the web, your message text is sent to a web search service to fetch results.

Data handling

  • Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 hashes.
  • You can delete any chat or memory at any time in the app; deletion removes it from the database.
  • Sessions use a signed, HTTP-only cookie.
  • We do not collect or send email. Because there is no email recovery, keep your password safe.
  • To delete your account and data entirely, contact the operator of this deployment.

API key security

  • A key is displayed once, when you create it. Only its SHA-256 hash is stored.
  • Treat keys like passwords: never put them in public code, client-side pages, screenshots or chats.
  • Revoke a key from the API keys page if you think it has leaked. Revocation is immediate.
  • You are responsible for all activity performed with your keys.

Acceptable use

Do not use Neural AI or its API to:

  • break the law or infringe others' rights;
  • generate malware, run scams or phishing, or harass, threaten or exploit people;
  • attempt to bypass rate limits, share keys to evade limits, or probe the service for weaknesses without permission;
  • send personal data of other people without a legitimate reason;
  • present AI output as professional medical, legal or financial advice.

We may suspend accounts or revoke keys that break these rules.

Account responsibilities

  • Keep your password private.
  • One person per account; do not impersonate others.
  • Keep your information accurate and tell us if you suspect unauthorized access.
  • AI replies can be wrong. Check important information yourself.

Rate limits and service limitations

  • Web chat has a daily request and input-size allowance per account (shown in the sidebar).
  • The API allows 30 requests per minute per key and 500 requests per day per account. Exceeding limits returns HTTP 429.
  • Replies have a maximum length, and requests have a maximum size.
  • The service is provided as is, without uptime guarantees. The AI model may be unavailable or slow, and limits may change.
